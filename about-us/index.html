About Clean Water Movers

A Nairobi-based moving and transport company handling house moves, office relocations and goods transport.

Who we are

Clean Water Movers is a moving and transport company based in Nairobi, serving households and businesses across Nairobi and Kiambu County.

We handle house moves, office relocations, furniture moving and goods transport, from single-item deliveries to full household and office moves. Alongside moving and transport, we also offer water tanker delivery as a secondary service.

  • Careful handling of furniture and belongings
  • Direct communication by phone and WhatsApp, no call centre
  • Flexible scope, from a single room to a full office
  • Serving Nairobi, Kiambu County and surrounding towns
