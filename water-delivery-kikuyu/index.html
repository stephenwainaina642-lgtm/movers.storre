Residential deliveries in Kikuyu

Households and landlords can enquire for deliveries to individual homes or shared tanks. If the property is within a gated compound, provide entry instructions and ensure someone can guide the tanker to the right receiving point.

Water for businesses and work sites

If the delivery is for a shop, office or construction site, mention the operating hours and any restrictions on vehicle access. Clear information helps establish whether the tanker can reach the receiving point and what arrangements may be required.

Check timing before you rely on it

Delivery availability varies with the route schedule and exact address. Contact us early, particularly when a delivery must fit a workday or a tank is close to empty. We will discuss the available option and quote before dispatch.
