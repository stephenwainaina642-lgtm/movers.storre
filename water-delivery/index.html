Clean Water Movers: Water Delivery in Nairobi & Kiambu County

Water tanker delivery for homes, apartments, offices, construction sites and institutions across Nairobi and Kiambu County — one-off or on a recurring schedule, with fast response when your tank runs low.

Order WaterCall 0720 792 185

Clean Water Movers delivers clean water by tanker to homes, apartments, offices, construction sites, schools, churches and other institutions across Nairobi and Kiambu County. Whether you need a single emergency delivery or a recurring supply, we dispatch a tanker sized to your tank and your schedule.

Water delivery in Nairobi can be unpredictable when mains supply is interrupted. We work with homeowners, landlords, site managers and business owners who need a dependable water tanker service they can call on directly — no call centre, no guesswork on timing.
